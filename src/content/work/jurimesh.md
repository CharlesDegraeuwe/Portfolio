## Overview

>Jurimesh is an AI legal tech company based in Ghent, building tools that automate due diligence for legal and M&A teams. 
> 
Traditional due diligence is slow and manual, requiring lawyers to read through massive volumes of contracts and documents to flag risks, 
obligations, and inconsistencies. 
Jurimesh uses AI to speed up that process, surfacing relevant clauses and risks automatically so legal teams can focus on judgment calls instead of document triage.


I've been working part-time as a full-stack developer on the team since April 2026. My work sits on the product side of the stack rather than the AI itself: I build and maintain the interfaces and APIs that legal teams actually use to review and interact with analyzed documents.

The work spans the full range of scope, anywhere from a three-line fix to a single PR with 8.000+ changes. 
On any given week that can mean small, well-defined tickets and bug fixes, or large overhauls where entire endpoints, frontend interfaces and components get rewritten from the ground up. 
On the frontend I work in the React application legal teams use, building complex, stateful interfaces for reviewing documents and keeping them fast and responsive under heavy data. 
On the backend I work on the Fastify and Rust services that power it, building type safe endpoints and wiring them up to the frontend. Most of the architecture is already in place or specced out in the tickets, 
so a lot of my job is implementing well-defined work cleanly, but every so often I get to make the more structural calls on a feature myself.

I work closely with the designer, usually translating his designs directly into the product, with a focus on getting the details and interactions right rather than approximating them.
I've also contributed to the design system, building shared, reusable components so the UI stays consistent across the app as it grows.

Since I started I've grown a lot, both technically and in the amount of ownership I'm trusted with, taking on progressively larger and more critical pieces of the product over time.
