## The problem

Students drown in material but lack a tutor that adapts to *how* they
learn. Generic AI chatbots answer questions, but they don't build a
model of the student, track weak spots, or structure a path forward.

## What I built

Studo is a study platform with an adaptable AI tutor at its core. It:

- builds a per-student knowledge model from their uploads and answers
- generates practice questions targeting weak areas
- explains concepts at the depth the student actually needs

## Stack

Built with **Next.js**, **Supabase**, and the **Claude API** for the
tutoring layer. Streaming responses keep the tutor feeling live.

## Status

Currently in active development — onboarding the first cohort of users.
